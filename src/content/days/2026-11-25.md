Rented skis, put my boots on, and immediately understood why everyone in Innsbruck walks like that.

The morning was snowplough school. A snowplough is less a technique and more a lifestyle: knees in, tips together, quiet panic. By lunch I could stop on purpose about half the time. The other half I stopped on the snow.

Last run of the day I made it down the whole nursery slope without stopping. Then got off the button lift and sat straight down. In front of everyone.
